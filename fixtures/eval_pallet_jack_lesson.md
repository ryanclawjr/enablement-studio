# EXAMPLE DATA — fictional warehouse lesson. Not from a live employer.

# Pallet-jack travel

Title: Pallet-jack safety

## Learning objective
Given a loaded pallet in a warehouse aisle, the learner will demonstrate
safe pallet-jack travel, as measured by a skills-lab checklist.

## Activity
Watch a twenty-minute video about company history, then post a selfie in
the break-room channel.

## Assessment
1. What time does the cafeteria open?
2. Name the break-room wifi password.
3. True or false: the suggestion box is by the vending machine.
