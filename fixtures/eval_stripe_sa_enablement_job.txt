Job title: Solution Architect Enablement Business Partner
Team: Global Sales Enablement

About the team
Global Sales Enablement drives productivity and effectiveness across the sales
organization. This role is a technical enablement expert who empowers solution
architects. The Solution Architect (SA) Enablement Business Partner develops
and delivers enablement programs that equip SA teams with product knowledge,
technical skills, and consultative selling capabilities for enterprise customers.

Responsibilities
- Design and deliver comprehensive onboarding programs, ongoing training, and
  just-in-time learning resources for SA teams
- Identify skill and knowledge gaps, then build enablement programs that drive
  measurable improvements in satisfaction, productivity, and revenue generation
- Package technical product information, solution architectures, and selling
  strategies for a sales audience using scalable content frameworks
- Establish metrics and analytics to track the impact of enablement initiatives
- Partner with product, engineering, and go-to-market teams so enablement
  content stays current for the field
- Partner with Product Operations and Product teams to stay ahead of upcoming
  product releases and ensure technical readiness prior to launch
- Cultivate a community of expert practitioners and user-generated content

Minimum requirements
- Experience in solution architecture and enablement for global organizations
  offering payments or SaaS-based solutions
- Ability to translate complex technical concepts for a sales audience
- Track record of designing and delivering enablement programs that drive
  measurable improvements in sales performance
- Ability to work with technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Experience with content creation, curation, and knowledge management
