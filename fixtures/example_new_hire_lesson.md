# Payments 101 (Harborline Payments — example)

Title: New-hire Payments 101

## Learning objective
By the end of this lesson, new hires will explain interchange, authorization,
and settlement to a small-business owner in plain language.

## Activity
Office scavenger hunt: find the coffee machine, the ping-pong table, and a
teammate who owns a dog. Post a selfie in the #new-hires Slack channel.

## Assessment
1. What floor is the all-hands held on?
2. Name three Slack channels you should join.
3. True or false: parking validation is at the front desk.
