Title: Warehouse pick, pack, and pallet-jack SOP
Site: Dock 4, invented fulfillment floor

Procedure

- Pack the carton, add the label, and close the flaps before it leaves the bench
- Move pallets with a pallet jack only after the travel path is clear
- Stop if a jack wheel binds; do not ride the jack in the aisle
- Teach-back: name the three stop points before the next lift
